How DropShop AR works

From browsing to buying, every step happens in your space — not just on your screen.

01

Browse in 3D

Every product ships as a real 3D model — rotate, zoom and inspect it from any angle before you decide.

02

Check the fit

Enter your room's width, length and height — DropShop measures the actual model and tells you if it fits.

03

Try it in AR

Launch your camera and place the piece life-size in your actual room with WebXR, Scene Viewer or Quick Look.

04

Compare & buy

Shortlist, compare up to three pieces side by side, then add your favorites to cart with confidence.
